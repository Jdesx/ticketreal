Every night out, kept.

Ticketreal is an iPhone app for people who go out. It finds the concerts, games and festivals in your camera roll and turns them into a diary, then lets you share the nights you choose with your friends.

How it works

From your camera roll to your friends.

Made for

However you go out.

Concerts

Every show you've seen, with the setlist. "Your 4th time seeing Movements" is counted for you.

Sports

The real final score of every game you were at, and your team's record with you in the building.

Festivals

A whole weekend as one night, with the lineup and every set you caught.

Trips and the rest

Road trips, a day out, a night worth keeping. Not everything has to be a show.

Your diary stays yours.

It lives on your phone and in your own iCloud. Only the nights you post go to Ticketreal, and only to the people you allow.

No ads. No tracking.

We don't sell or share what you do. Photos are sent without their location.

Friends, not followers.

Accounts start private. Friends see your posts; strangers see nothing unless you say so.
