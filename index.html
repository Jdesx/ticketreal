It finds your nights for you.

Seenit looks at when and where your photos were taken and suggests the shows, games and festivals you were at. Pick one and the details fill themselves in.

No photos? Scan the ticket, or type in the venue and Seenit finds the show.

2

Post the nights you want, with the photos you pick.

Tap your favorite photos and clips. Only those go up, without their location. Your account starts private, so only friends see what you post.

3

See where everyone went.

Scroll the nights your friends post, with the score or the setlist right on top. Like them, comment, and see who else was there.

Start a group for the season.

Make one for your team, your concert crew, or just you and a friend. The board ranks who went out most, and who's the lucky charm when the team wins.
