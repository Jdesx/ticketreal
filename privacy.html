Ticketreal

Privacy

Last updated October 28, 2026. Ticketreal is in beta.

Ticketreal turns your camera roll into a diary of the shows, games and trips you've been to, and lets you share the nights you choose with friends. This page says exactly what it keeps, where, and who can see it. It's written to be read, not to cover us.

Your diary stays yours

Your nights (the events you log, your tickets, who you went with, setlists and scores) are stored on your phone and in your own iCloud, in a private database that only your Apple ID can open. We can't read it. Your photos and videos stay in your camera roll; Ticketreal reads them on your phone to match them to your nights and doesn't copy them anywhere unless you post a night.

What we keep on our server

Only what you choose to share, and what's needed to share it:

  • Your account: an identifier from Sign in with Apple, and the name Apple shares when you first sign in. We ask Apple for your name only, not your email. If Apple provides an email or private-relay address, we don't use it.
  • Your profile: your handle, display name, photo, bio, profile theme, and whether your account is public or private (it starts private).
  • setlist.fm, if you add it: your setlist.fm username and the totals from the shows you've marked "I was there" on setlist.fm (shows, acts, venues and your most-seen acts), for your profile to show. Remove it from your profile and it's gone.
  • Friends and safety: who you're friends with, friend requests, who you've blocked, and reports you file.
  • Nights you post: the act, venue, date and caption; smaller copies of the night's photos and the videos you pick; which ones are your picks. Copies are made fresh on your phone before upload, so they carry no location or other photo metadata. Originals never leave your phone.
  • What people do with posted nights: likes and comments.
  • Your stats: the totals and short lists on your Concerts, Sports and Travel pages (counts, the acts and teams you've seen most, your record), worked out on your phone. Only the ones you leave switched on in Edit profile are sent; habits that say when or where to find you (your go-out night, busiest month, home turf, last outing) start switched off. The nights behind the numbers stay on your phone.
  • Groups: the groups you make or join, their chat, and your totals for each group (counts like shows this year, a team's record with you there, and a list of acts you've seen). The nights behind those totals stay on your phone.

Our server is a Supabase database in the United States. Every table checks who's asking before it answers, so the database itself enforces the rules below, not just the app.

Who can see what

  • Friends see every photo of a night you post, plus the videos you picked, unless you hide that night from friends.
  • Private accounts (the default) are hidden from Discover and from search. Nobody but your friends sees your nights or stats. People become your friend only through a link you send or by typing your exact handle.
  • Public accounts can be found in Discover and search. Anyone on Ticketreal can see your stats and the picks from nights you post, and your picks can be recommended to them in Discover and Clips. The rest of each night stays with your friends.
  • Your diary is never shown in Discover, public or private. Only nights you post leave your phone.
  • Group members see your totals for that group and its chat, never your nights.
  • Blocking someone hides you from each other completely. They aren't told.
  • Reports are read only by the people who run Ticketreal. To review one, they can see the night, comment or message it's about, and remove it.

Messages

Direct messages between friends are stored in Ticketreal's iCloud database (Apple's CloudKit), with the sender, the recipient and the text, so Apple can deliver message notifications. Only the two people in a conversation see it in the app.

Looking things up

When you search for a show, artist, team or venue, the text you type is sent to the service that answers it: setlist.fm (lineups and setlists), MusicBrainz (artist names), TheSportsDB (games and scores), Apple Maps (venues), and Deezer, Wikidata and Apple Music (artist photos). Nothing else about you goes with it.

Most of these lookups go through our server, which adds our own key for the service. To stop abuse it keeps a count of lookups per account (or, before you sign in, per internet address) for a day, and nothing about what you searched for.

Location is used on your phone, while the app is open, to suggest the venue you're at. It isn't sent to us.

Your music app

If you connect Apple Music, the app reads the songs in your library and their play counts, on your phone, to show who you listen to most and how an act you've seen ranks for you. It stays on your phone; we never receive it. A few beta testers can link Spotify instead, which reads your top artists and songs from Spotify the same way, and keeps the sign-in in your phone's keychain. Disconnect either from your Listening page.

Sharing outside Ticketreal

When you share a card or a clip, it's made on your phone and handed to the app you choose: Instagram or Facebook Stories, Messages, your camera roll, or anything else in the share sheet. For Instagram and Facebook, the picture or video and a link to Ticketreal are passed to that app on your phone, and from then on Meta's own privacy policy applies. We don't send anything to Meta ourselves and don't use any Meta tracking.

During the beta: how you use the app, if you say yes

After you pick a handle, the app asks once whether it can record how you use it while it's in beta. If you say yes, it records which screens you open and for how long, which features you use (posting, liking, groups, sharing and so on), and how long each visit lasts, with the app's build number. It never records your photos, messages, comments, captions, searches, or anything you type. You can turn it off any time in Settings › Beta. If you send feedback from Settings, we keep what you wrote.

This goes to our own database, not to an analytics company, and only the people who run Ticketreal can read it. Deleting your account deletes it.

What we don't do

  • No ads, and no ad or tracking libraries.
  • We don't sell or rent your information, or share it with anyone not listed on this page.
  • No analytics company, and no usage recording unless you said yes to it during the beta.

Deleting your account

Settings › Account › Delete Account removes your account and everything on our server: your profile, stats, friends, blocks, posted nights and their photos and videos, likes, comments and group data. It also erases your diary from your phone and your iCloud. It can't be undone. Messages you sent to a friend remain in that conversation for them.

Signing out keeps your diary on your phone and doesn't delete anything.

Age

During the beta, Ticketreal is for people 18 and over.
